GTM Deal Pipeline · Pillar 4 · processes and tiering

The POC covers the first three stages, and stops at the gate

The official sales process runs four stages under their own SLAs, with a Go/No-Go gate closing three of them. The three agents in this tool automate Lead Qualification, Discovery and Solution Shaping; Estimation and Closing are still manual work.

POC coverage

Stage → app tab

  • Lead Qualification → 1. Scoring
  • Discovery → 2. Discovery
  • Solution Shaping & Validation → 3. Solution Shaping
  • Estimation & Proposal → not yet (the Proceed to Estimation button is reserved)
  • Deal Support & Close → out of POC scope
  • CRM handoff artifacts: Open Case enters Discover, Closed Ticket exits Closing

Three gates in the official process

What each gate decides

  • Possibility — closes the Leads stage on a warm call + BANTI
  • Solution Fit (PPS) — closes the Discover stage on mapping, risk and F³
  • Competitiveness — closes the Propose stage on resource and tier

Prospects tiering · MB = million THB

F³ sets the tier

  • Tier 1 · template · >1 MB · BizDomain
  • Tier 2 · story-driven · 1–3 MB · Biz + SolDomain
  • Tier 3 · strategic C-level · >5 MB · GTM + SolDomain
